Scope 4 & Scope 5 applicability with respect to the Baseline Scenario –

 Prior to 2019, the Project Proponent had an operational ETP with the treated effluent being
discharged to Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation (AMC) sewers in compliance with the
extant legal framework and its production borewell (groundwater) remained its primary
source of water.

 In 2019, the Project Proponent set up a STP and entered into a contract with the AMC to
procure corporation untreated sewage from its distant collection sites (sites being scattered
all over the city of Ahmedabad outside the premises of the Project Proponent) and carry out
treatment of the untreated sewage so procured in its on site STP for use in production process
thus reducing its reliance on ground water.

 Further, in 2022, the unit was converted into ZLD with upgradations in its existing ETP thus
further reducing its reliance on ground water.

 These developments thus establish its compliance with the baseline scenario parameters laid
down in UWR RoU Standard 8.1 and further establish qualification of the project under Scope
4 & Scope 5 for RoUs under the Standard. Also refer Annexure to this report for sample
evidences supporting its procurement of untreated corporation sewage as described above.
